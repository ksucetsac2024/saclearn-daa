PCCST502 · Design and Analysis of Algorithms

Learn every algorithm by watching it run

All 25 topics of the KTU syllabus, one page each. Every page explains the idea from the ground up, then gives you a stepper you can drive with your own input, so you can check your hand-worked MQP answers against it.

Read, then run

Each topic opens with the idea and the pseudocode. The blue "Try it" panels run the algorithm one step at a time with an explanation under each step.

Use your own numbers

Every stepper takes your input. Solve a question on paper first, then type it in and step through to find exactly where you went wrong.

Check, then tick

Each topic ends with a short quiz. Mark the topic done; your ticks are kept in this browser.

Exam pattern

Part A: 8 questions × 3 marks, two from each module, all compulsory. Part B: one 9-mark question per module, chosen from two, with up to three sub-parts. Total 60 marks in 2½ hours.
